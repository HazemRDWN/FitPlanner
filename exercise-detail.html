Barbell Bench Press

Chest

Barbell

Intermediate

SETS x REPS

4 x 6-10

REST

90 s

BEST

80 kg

Instructions

1

Lie flat, eyes under the bar, feet planted, shoulder blades squeezed.

Lie flat on the bench with eyes under the bar, feet planted and shoulder blades squeezed together.

2

Grip slightly wider than shoulders and unrack with straight arms.

Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width and unrack it with straight arms over your chest.

3

Lower under control to mid-chest, elbows about 45°.

Inhale and lower the bar under control to mid-chest, elbows at roughly 45° to your body.

4

Press up until arms are straight; exhale at the top.

Press the bar back up and slightly toward your face until your arms are straight. Exhale at the top.

5

Re-rack by moving the bar back to the hooks first.

Repeat for the planned reps, then re-rack the bar by moving it back to the hooks before bending your elbows.
